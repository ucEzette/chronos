ONEROAD – The Decentralized Gumroad Killer | Encrypted Digital Marketplace on Arc

Introducing ONEROAD on Arc

ONEROAD is a fully decentralized peer-to-peer marketplace for digital assets – think Gumroad, but built entirely on-chain with end-to-end encryption and no middleman taking 10%+ of your earnings.

We're building the future of digital commerce on Arc Network because we believe creators deserve 100% ownership of their sales, instant settlement, and true privacy.


Why We Call It the "Gumroad Killer"

Gumroad charges 10% + processing fees → ONEROAD charges 0% platform fee

Gumroad payment settlement takes 1-7 days → ONEROAD pays instantly on-chain

Gumroad can ban creators/products → ONEROAD is unstoppable and decentralized

Gumroad stores files on centralized servers → ONEROAD encrypts everything end-to-end with AES-256

Gumroad controls your store → You own your listings on-chain

Gumroad requires KYC → ONEROAD is wallet-based, no signup needed

The problem with Gumroad: Creators have been banned, had payments frozen, and lost access to their audience overnight. Centralized platforms = centralized control.

ONEROAD's solution: Your files are encrypted before upload. Only buyers with valid on-chain purchases can decrypt. No one – not even us – can access your content or take down your store.


How It Works

1. Connect Wallet – No signup, no email, no KYC
2. Create Listing – Upload encrypted file + preview, set price in USDC/tokens
3. Publish to Chain – Listing minted as on-chain asset
4. Instant Payment – Buyer pays, seller receives instantly via smart contract
5. Decryption Key Delivered – Buyer's wallet receives the key to unlock their purchase


Tech Stack & Why We're Decentralized

Frontend: Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Wagmi + Viem for Web3, IPFS via Pinata for decentralized storage

Smart Contracts: Solidity (ERC-721 based listings), deployed on Arc Testnet & DataHaven

Why Decentralized?
• No single point of failure – Files on IPFS, logic on-chain
• Censorship-resistant – No company can remove your listings
• Trustless escrow – Smart contracts handle payments, not a company
• Verifiable ownership – Every transaction is public and auditable
• Privacy-first – AES-256 encryption means only buyers see content


Why Arc Network?

We chose to build on Arc for several key reasons:

1. USDC-Native Transactions – Stablecoin payments mean creators aren't exposed to volatility
2. Low Fees – More of every sale goes to the creator
3. Developer-Friendly – Excellent tooling and documentation
4. Growing Ecosystem – Aligned with Arc's vision for real-world use cases
5. Circle Partnership – Trust and legitimacy from day one
6. Fast Finality – Transactions settle in seconds, not minutes

Arc is the perfect home for a creator economy platform where people actually need to receive real money for their work.
